Kat's Creations AI builds software people can open and use right away. Every project starts as a single file, so there is nothing to install, no account to create, and nothing standing between you and the thing itself. Games, tools, and experiments all live under the same roof, made by one person who writes the code, designs the look, and decides what ships.

The catalog moves fast. There is a 3D world building style voxel sandbox, a pixel art tower defense game about defending a garden from zombies, a comic book racing game with its own radio stations, and a driving game set in a small coastal town. Beyond games, there is an internet radio station with a live dashboard and a storefront built for selling digital goods. Some projects bring in Gemini through bring your own key, so your data and your costs stay in your hands.

I am based in Ocean Shores, Washington, and run on curiosity, long nights, and a stubborn belief that independent creators can build things that feel finished without a big team behind them. Nothing here is a mockup. Everything works, everything is made with care, and everything is made by me, Katt.

Aside from games and things, I also build and train custom AI! :)
